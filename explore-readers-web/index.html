Explore Reader’s Web

Here I collect links to websites that support Reader’s Web. Once you install the plugin on your website, send me the link and I’ll feature your website here.

You can replace your current theme with the Reader UI. Alternatively, you can keep your existing theme, but your readers will need to install the extension to see the Reader UI. In that case, you may want to add a note explaining that the extension is required, especially on pages where you use visible connections.
